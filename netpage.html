Conference/Journal 2023

MixScene: A Benchmark for Text-Driven Audio Effects and Mixing on Multi-Stem Audio

Zhechun Liang, Cuiling Lan, Tao Huang, Yuxin Feng, Zhenyu Wang, Fangfang Wu, Weisheng Dong
Xidian University, MSRA
NIPS 2027
Paper Supplementary Code arXiv

Abstract

Sound designers, podcast and video producers, and novice users increasingly want to specify how a recorded audio scene should sound in natural language rather than manually setting effect-chain parameters or mix decisions. We study this as text-driven audio effects and mixing for multi-stem recordings: given a free-form scene description and raw audio stems, a system renders a stereo mix that coordinates effects, balance, and spatial placement to realise the described scene. We introduce MixScene, a new benchmark to facilitate this study. Particularly, we simulate the audio effects and mixing requirements from four key aspects: scene setting, subjective feeling, spatial distribution, and volume balance, to cover diverse acoustic intentions. We also introduce reference-free evaluation metrics: an objective CLAP text-audio similarity score and a semantic-consistency score. We further propose Dual-memory Executable Mixing Agents (DEMA), a multi-agent framework that casts scene rendering as executable mixing programs: a Planner Agent proposes effect chains and mix decisions, a Debug Agent repairs non-executable programs by querying Debug Memory with the runtime error, and an Experience Agent refines parameters by querying Experience Memory with Critic Agent feedback. Compared with other baselines, DEMA achieves superior performance. We hope that this benchmark facilitates further studies for text-driven audio production.
